Check waiting time for a stable first-order process or sensor response. Choose input units and calculate one value within the stated conditions.

Calculations run in this browser. Scientific notation such as 1.2e-3 is supported. Do not group thousands.

Result

First-order settling time t_s

—

Enter values and select Calculate.

FORMULA

t_s = −τ ln(ε)

Assumptions

Use τ>0 and 0<ε<1. This is the exact stable first-order unit-step response with no pure delay or overshoot. Entering 2% means ε=0.02. Do not apply a blanket 4τ rule to systems with multiple poles.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Measurement uncertainty and design safety factors are not calculated.
